Metodologi backtest (freqtrade-style)

• Data dinormalisasi: sort timestamp, dedup, validasi OHLC finite + high/low konsisten, filter tanggal.
• Sinyal dihitung di close[i], eksekusi di open[i+1] + slippage merugikan — tanpa lookahead.
• SL/TP intrabar: bila keduanya tersentuh dalam 1 candle → SL dulu (konservatif).
• Fee dihitung 2 sisi (entry+exit) × fee%, slippage 2 sisi.
• Sizing: risiko tetap (equity × risk%) / jarak SL, notional di-clamp ke equity × leverage. Leverage 1–100, risiko 0.1–10%.
• 1 posisi per waktu (non-overlap), equity compounding, max holding → EXPIRED.
• Metrik: winRate, profitFactor (aman bagi-nol), expectancy, avgWin/Loss, averageR, maxDD + %, Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, CAGR, exposure, streaks.
